FAQs

Candidate FAQs

What's the typical process after I apply?+

We start with a short screening call to confirm your skills and credentials, followed by client interviews and document verification. If it's a match, we support you through the offer and onboarding.

What documents will I need?+

Typically a resume/CV, government-issued ID, and any role-relevant licenses, certifications, or transcripts. The exact list depends on the role — your recruiter will confirm what's needed.

Do you support international placements?+

Yes, for select nursing, physician, and clinical roles across the USA, UK, EU, Middle East, and APAC. We provide general relocation support guidance, but recommend confirming visa and immigration requirements with official resources.

Is there a fee to apply?+

No. There is never a fee to job seekers to apply, interview, or be placed through Suprim Global Health.
